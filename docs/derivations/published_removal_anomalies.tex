\begin{center}\large Adjusting published removal anomalies\end{center}
Let $Q_j>0$ denote annual methane removal by OH, $m_j$ the atmospheric
burden, and $k_j=Q_j/m_j$ the effective loss frequency. The reference year is
2019, denoted by subscript 0. He's OH forcing is
\begin{equation}
F_{{\rm OH},j}=-m_0(k_j-k_0)=Q_0-Q_jm_0/m_j.
\end{equation}
Qu (2022), Table 1, supplies both $Q$ and $m$. We use their ratio consistently,
rather than mixing it with the separately reported lifetime column. Its other
removal combines chemical and soil sinks, which cannot be separated into
He's two terms from the table.

Ciais (2026), Figure S3, plots signed sink anomalies $S=-Q$ relative to 2010.
Subtracting each curve's 2019 value gives
\begin{equation}
s_j=S_j-S_0=Q_0-Q_j.
\end{equation}
The 2010 reference cancels. Native $Q_0$ and annual burdens are not tabulated.
For a conditional comparison we prescribe the NOAA surface-mean burden proxy
and an OH-only lifetime $\tau_{{\rm OH},0}$:
\begin{equation}
Q_0=\frac{m_0}{\tau_{{\rm OH},0}},\qquad
F_{{\rm OH},j}=\frac{m_0}{m_j}
\left(s_j+\frac{m_j-m_0}{\tau_{{\rm OH},0}}\right).
\end{equation}
This sensitivity calculation does not recover Ciais's native forcing. We use
11.1 yr as a benchmark and 9--12 yr as a sensitivity range; 11.1 yr is He's
reported mean OH lifetime, not a known Ciais 2019 value. Coordinate-reading
precision is separate from inversion spread. The annual circles are read
at their published year labels.

For a uniform fractional OH reduction $r$, with unchanged temperature and
baseline weighting, the prescribed scenario gives
\begin{equation}
k_j=(1-r)k_0,\qquad F_{{\rm OH},j}=r\,m_0/\tau_{{\rm OH},0}.
\end{equation}
Laughner's 2--4\% range motivates $r$; it is a temporal sensitivity envelope,
not a posterior confidence interval. The reduction is applied during 2020
only. Returning the imposed forcing to zero in 2021--2022 does not remove
the burden accumulated in 2020. This calculation does not reproduce
Laughner's hemispheric trajectories or their 2021 extrapolations.
All flux terms have units Tg CH$_4$ yr$^{-1}$ and burdens Tg CH$_4$.
